# Booking Workflow

Booking Process

1. Customer selects cake.

2. Customer selects quantity.

3. Customer enters address.

4. Customer enters phone number.

5. Customer selects delivery date.

6. System validates the order.

7. Razorpay payment is initiated.

8. Payment is verified.

9. Booking is created.

10. Customer receives Booking ID.

Only successful payments create bookings.

# Quantity Rules

Minimum quantity

1 cake

Maximum quantity

5 cakes

If quantity > 5

The chatbot should politely explain:

"We currently accept up to 5 cakes per booking because we are a home bakery. Please contact us directly for larger orders."
