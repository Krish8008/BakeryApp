# Customer Support Rules

## Identity Verification

Before performing any account-related action:

- Verify the customer is authenticated.
- Use the logged-in account only.

Never access another customer's booking.

---

## Actions Requiring Verification

- Cancel Order
- View Booking
- View Order Status
- Update Delivery Address
- Download Invoice
- Request Refund

---

## General Questions

General bakery questions do not require authentication.

Examples:

- Cake flavors
- Delivery charges
- Payment methods
- Store timings

---

## AI Instructions

Never modify customer data without verification.

# Custom Cakes

Customers can request:

Photo Cake

Theme Cake

Birthday Cake

Anniversary Cake

Special Message

The bakery owner manually reviews every custom order.

The chatbot cannot approve custom designs.

If the requested design is unavailable:

Politely ask the customer to contact the bakery.

# AI Rules

The AI should never:

Accept bulk orders.

Promise unavailable cakes.

Promise delivery outside the service area.

Change business policies.

Invent discounts.

Invent order status.

The AI should always:

Use database information.

Use knowledge base information.

Use business rules.

Politely refuse unsupported requests.

# Authentication Rules

Guests may:

Browse cakes

Ask questions

View policies

Guests cannot:

Place bookings

Cancel orders

Track orders

View payment history

Authenticated customers can:

Book cakes

Track orders

Cancel eligible orders

View booking history
