# Payment Policy

## Accepted Payment Methods

We accept secure online payments through:

- UPI
- Credit Cards
- Debit Cards
- Net Banking
- Wallets
- Razorpay Supported Payment Methods

---

## Payment Security

All online payments are processed securely through Razorpay.

Sweet Bakery never stores:
- Credit card numbers
- Debit card details
- CVV
- Banking passwords
- UPI PIN

Your payment information is encrypted and processed securely.

---

## Order Confirmation

After successful payment:

1. Payment is verified.
2. Your order is confirmed.
3. A confirmation message/email is sent.
4. Your order appears in your account dashboard.

---

## Failed Payments

If payment fails:

- No order is created.
- No amount is charged (in most cases).
- If money is deducted, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank.

If the amount is not reversed within 5–7 business days, contact customer support.

---

## Payment Pending

Sometimes payments remain pending due to:

- Bank server issues
- Network interruption
- Payment gateway timeout

If payment is successful but the order is not confirmed, please contact support with:

- Payment ID
- Transaction Screenshot
- Registered Email

---

## Refund Policy

Refunds are available only in eligible situations, such as:

- Duplicate payment
- Payment deducted but order not created
- Bakery cancelled the order
- Product unavailable

Refunds are generally not provided for:

- Incorrect delivery address provided by customer
- Customer unavailable during delivery
- Last-minute cancellation after preparation has started
- Customized cakes that have already been prepared

---

## Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within:

- UPI: 2–5 Business Days
- Debit Card: 5–7 Business Days
- Credit Card: 5–7 Business Days
- Net Banking: 5–7 Business Days
- Wallet: Depending on wallet provider

---

## Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled only before preparation begins.

Customized cakes may not be cancelled once baking or decoration has started.

---

## Duplicate Payments

If multiple payments are made accidentally for the same order:

- Contact customer support immediately.
- After verification, the extra payment will be refunded.

---

## Taxes

All applicable taxes are included in the final order amount unless stated otherwise.

---

## Payment Issues

If you experience any payment problems, please contact customer support with:

- Order ID
- Payment ID
- Registered Email
- Screenshot of the transaction

Our support team will assist you as quickly as possible.
