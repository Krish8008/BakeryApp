# Sweet Bakery Business Rules

Sweet Bakery is a small-scale home bakery.

The bakery accepts orders only within the local delivery area.

The bakery prepares cakes after receiving confirmed orders.

Maximum production capacity:

• Maximum 5 cake orders per day.

Bulk commercial orders are not accepted.

Business Hours

Monday – Sunday

9:00 AM – 8:00 PM

Customers can place orders at any time, but order processing starts during business hours.
