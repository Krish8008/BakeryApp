# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General

### What is Sweet Bakery?
Sweet Bakery is an online bakery specializing in fresh cakes, pastries, cupcakes, desserts, and custom celebration cakes made with premium ingredients.

### What are your business hours?
We are open every day from **9:00 AM to 9:00 PM**.

### Where do you deliver?
We currently deliver within our supported delivery areas. Enter your address during checkout to check availability.

### How can I contact customer support?
You can contact us through:
- Live Chat
- Contact Form
- Email
- Phone
- WhatsApp (if available)

---

## Orders

### How do I place an order?
1. Browse cakes.
2. Select your favorite cake.
3. Choose size, flavor, and customization.
4. Add to cart.
5. Complete payment.
6. Receive order confirmation.

### Can I order for a future date?
Yes. You can select your preferred delivery date and time during checkout.

### What is the minimum order value?
There is no minimum order value unless mentioned during promotions.

### Can I place a bulk order?
Yes. We accept bulk and corporate orders. Contact our support team for special pricing.

### Can I order multiple cakes together?
Yes. You can add multiple cakes to your cart before checkout.

---

## Custom Cakes

### Do you make custom birthday cakes?
Yes. We make customized cakes for:
- Birthdays
- Weddings
- Anniversaries
- Baby Showers
- Corporate Events
- Festivals

### How much advance notice is required?
- Regular custom cakes: 24–48 hours
- Large or themed cakes: 3–7 days

### Can I upload a reference image?
Yes. You can upload an image while placing your custom cake order.

---

## Delivery

### Do you offer same-day delivery?
Same-day delivery is available for selected cakes depending on availability.

### Can I schedule delivery?
Yes. Choose your preferred delivery slot during checkout.

### What happens if no one is available during delivery?
Our delivery partner will contact the recipient. If delivery cannot be completed, our support team will assist with rescheduling when possible.

### Can I track my order?
Yes. Order status can be viewed from your account dashboard.

Possible order statuses:
- Pending
- Confirmed
- Preparing
- Out for Delivery
- Delivered
- Cancelled
